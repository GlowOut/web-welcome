Past, present and future appointments in one place — including invoices.

Client Books

Team & Employees

Add staff, manage their diaries and see every service they’ve carried out.

Unlimited Services

Your Own Gallery

Showcase your best work with unlimited photos that help win new clients.

Client & Revenue Reports

See who booked what, when — and download income reports as PDF or CSV.

Client Reviews

Link Your Socials

Courses & Classes

Glowy AI Assistant

AI Menu & Form Import

Consultation Forms

Gift Cards

Send branded gift cards by email — clients redeem them when they book.

Waitlists

Multi-Location

Service Packages

SMS Reminders

Send text reminders alongside email and push — fewer forgotten appointments.
