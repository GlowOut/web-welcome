Everything you need to run the business

Bookings, payments, clients, team and AI — all included. No commission, no hidden charges. Want something extra? Tell us.

Bookings & diary

Your day, in one place.

  • Booking calendar — past, present and future appointments, including invoices.
  • Unlimited services — photos, duration and pricing; hide any when you pause.
  • Courses & classes — group sessions with dates, capacity and pricing.
  • Service packages — bundle treatments for bridal, courses and bestsellers.
  • Waitlists — notify clients when a slot opens and convert in a tap.
  • Flexible hours — holidays and dynamic working times that match real life.

Payments & protection

Get paid. Keep the diary.

  • Card payments — Stripe checkout when clients book.
  • Deposits — fixed amount or percentage so no-shows don’t cost the day.
  • No-show protection — keep deposits when clients don’t turn up.
  • Klarna — let clients pay later, you still get paid.
  • Gift cards — branded cards by email, redeemed at booking.
  • Promotions & coupons — run offers without the spreadsheet.

Clients & team

Solo or a full salon.

  • Client books — history, upcoming visits and contact details, automatically.
  • Consultation forms — build, assign to services, store replies on the booking.
  • Reviews — collect trust-building feedback, with reporting when you need it.
  • Team logins — add staff, manage diaries and see every service they did.
  • Multi-location — several branches, one app, organised teams.
  • Unlimited clients & team — no per-seat tax as you grow.

Grow & automate

Look bigger than a diary app.

  • Partner panel — revenue, top services and what’s coming up next.
  • Reports — who booked what, when — download as PDF or CSV.
  • Gallery & socials — showcase work and link Facebook or Instagram.
  • Glowy AI — chat or talk to book, reschedule, manage clients and waitlists.
  • AI import — photograph a price list or paper form, get editable drafts.
  • Your booking link — listed on GlowOut, plus email, push and SMS reminders.
